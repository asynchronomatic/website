The big guys will rent you intelligence by the token. That works until the bill, the outage, or the terms change. Asynchromatic is software for the other path: **AI at scale on machines you already own.**

No frontier lab in the middle. No cloud that keeps the weights. A group stands up its own floor — local models, private traffic, timing you can trust — and grows from there.

The name is the method. **Async** is how work flows: non-blocking, event-driven, ready when the world is. **Chronomatic** is how it keeps time: clocks, schedules, consensus. Together they are Asynchromatic: asynchronous intelligence with chronometric precision.

Speakeasy is the first room in the house. Share local Ollama models with a private group, behind one OpenAI-compatible endpoint. Clients you already use keep talking to a local proxy. The mesh never asks a hyperscaler for a completion.

More rooms will open. The point is not a single booth. The point is a stack you can run without asking permission.
